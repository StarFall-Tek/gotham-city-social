01 — Services

A site, a social presence, and a way to be found.

Most shops and studios don’t need a platform. They need a page that says what they do, a profile that isn’t blank, and a phone number that matches both.

  1. 01

    Websites

    Pages cover services, photos, hours, and a way to get in touch. Phone first, because that is where people look.

  2. 02

    Social setup

    Bios, profile setup, and a posting rhythm you can keep. The accounts should sound like the same business as the site.

  3. 03

    Local search

    Page titles, a short description, and a Google Business profile so someone nearby can find you by name or by trade.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    You leave with the logins, a short walkthrough, and notes on how to change a photo or a price. The site is yours.

  1. 01

    Checkup

    Fifteen minutes on what you have now. You’ll hear what is working, what is missing, and what should be built first.

  2. 02

    Build

    The pages, the profiles, and the contact paths, in the scope we agreed.

  3. 03

    Handoff

    A live walkthrough and a checklist, so you can update the site on your own.
