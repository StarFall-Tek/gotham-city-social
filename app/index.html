Install

Add it to your home screen

It takes about ten seconds. The app opens full-screen with the Gotham City Social icon, like any other app.

iPhone & iPad

Safari

  1. Open gotham-city-social.netlify.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

Chrome

  1. Open gotham-city-social.netlify.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app above, or the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Desktop

Chrome or Edge

  1. Open the site in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Click Install.

App Store & Google Play: coming soon. There is no store listing yet. Until then, this installable web app is the official Gotham City Social app.
